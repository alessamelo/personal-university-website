My Weekly Schedule

This is my weekly class schedule for the 8th semester of Computer Science at Yachay Tech.

I have organized my schedule to balance my university responsibilities with my personal needs. In particular, I try to leave enough time for my medical appointments while still having enough time to attend my classes, study, and complete my university work.

Keeping a structured schedule helps me manage my time and maintain a balance between my studies and other responsibilities throughout the semester.

My Schedule

Optimization Methods: combines theoretical and practical classes focused on mathematical and computational methods for solving optimization problems. We work with different techniques and apply them to practical exercises during class.

Web Applications: includes both theoretical and practical activities. On Tuesdays, we have theoretical classes where we learn concepts related to web development, while Fridays are focused on workshop activities where we put those concepts into practice using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and jQuery.

Scientific Communication: focuses on active communication and participation during class. We work on different tasks and activities related to communicating scientific and technical ideas. Occasionally, when the professor has a scheduling conflict, some tasks are assigned to be completed independently.

Intelligent Agents: focuses on artificial intelligence and intelligent systems. The classes include different activities related to the topics covered in the course, as well as occasional assignments that allow us to apply what we have learned.

Thesis Session: dedicated to working on my thesis project, including planning, research, development, and reviewing my progress.

Bass Class: a weekly personal activity where I continue developing my skills in playing bass.
